01 / The story

Coastal calm.
A warm welcome.

Crow’s Nest offers self-catering accommodation in Saldanha Bay. Its brand story centres on comfort, simplicity and a sense of home on the West Coast.

The identity translates that welcome into a recognisable emblem, a coastal palette and consistent materials for introducing the accommodation.

02 / The mark

A crow.
A nest. A connection.

The curved “C” serves as both the brand’s initial and a protective frame around the crow. The guide connects this shape to a nest: a place of comfort and belonging.

The emblem works alone at smaller sizes, while the full wordmark and Saldanha Bay line establish the name and location.

03 / The visual language

Drawn from
the coast.

Deep sea blues anchor the identity. Greens and a yellow-green accent introduce freshness, while off-white gives the system a softer background.

The guide specifies Telugu MN as the primary typeface. Its character complements the emblem and gives the brand a distinct voice across its communications.

04 / The company profile

An introduction
to the stay.

The company profile brings the identity together with photographs of the accommodation. The layout introduces the story, units and amenities through clear sections, generous imagery and the brand’s blue and green accents.

05 / The website

Explore the rooms.
Plan the stay.

The live website carries the brand into the guest journey, pairing the crow emblem and coastal colours with photographs of the accommodation.

Visitors can explore room types, browse the gallery and follow the booking links. The homepage gives those actions a clear place alongside the property’s story.
