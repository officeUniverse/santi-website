01 / THE IDENTITY

A strong name.
A clear presence.

Guildco operates across construction, property development and mining services. The identity gives that broad offering a consistent visual presence, from a first introduction to a detailed company profile.

A bold wordmark anchors the system. A compact band of colour and oversized outline forms add recognition without competing with the company name.

02 / THE SYSTEM

Structure, with
character.

The starter guide pairs the Montserrat family with near-black, forest green, teal, earthy brown and a light neutral. These elements give the brand a practical vocabulary for headings, page structure and graphic accents.

Vertical colour bands and outline letterforms connect the materials while allowing photography and content to take the lead.

03 / IN PRINT

Small details.
A lasting impression.

The business-card artwork carries the same wordmark, narrow colour band and large outline motif into a compact format. The restrained front places the brand first.

04 / COMPANY PROFILES

One identity.
A broader story.

The supplied profiles show how the system extends across construction and mining communications. Large industrial photographs introduce each document, while clear headings and consistent accents organise the service information.

The later mining profile uses colour panels and modular layouts to make a more detailed offering easier to navigate.

05 / THE WEBSITE

The brand,
working online.

The live website brings the identity into a digital setting, combining the white wordmark and dark navigation with construction imagery and green and teal accents.

Dedicated routes to Construction and Mining Services help visitors find the relevant offering, alongside company information and contact details.
