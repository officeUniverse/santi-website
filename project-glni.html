01 / The brief

Its own voice.
A shared mission.

GLNI needed a dedicated international website, separate from the Global Leadership Summit site. A home for its story, its programmes and the people connecting its network around the world.

Santi handled strategy and website design, then continued into ongoing maintenance and client website support. The challenge was to create a distinct international presence while retaining a clear connection to the wider GLN identity.

Visit the website ↗

Desktop homepage

02 / The experience

Designed around
people and purpose.

The mountain mark and blue accents connect the website to the wider GLN brand. Confident typography, event imagery and clear navigation bring the international organisation’s story into focus.

From the homepage to the About page, the design gives visitors clear routes into the organisation, its Summit experiences and its network.

03 / The network

A world of
connections.

The centrepiece is an interactive globe. Visitors can rotate it, explore countries and find the people leading local GLN initiatives. A country directory offers another route to those connections.

Explore the live globe ↗

04 / Beyond launch

Built to connect.
Supported to grow.

The relationship continues through updates, backups, security maintenance, fixes and website changes as the client’s needs develop.

GLNI supplies the French and Spanish translations. Santi maintains the website experience across those languages alongside English, keeping design and ongoing care connected.
