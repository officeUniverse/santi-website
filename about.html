Our story

We don’t just
build websites

Santi Universe is a web design, graphic design and AI engineering studio based in Johannesburg, South Africa (Roodepoort, Gauteng), with a growing footprint in Kenya and clients around the world. We craft stunning, high-performance websites that drive real results — blending design, functionality and the latest AI technology to turn visitors into customers.

From eCommerce stores and custom web apps to brand identities, AI assistants and answer-engine optimisation (AEO), we build digital solutions that empower your business — whether you’re in Johannesburg, Nairobi, or anywhere else in the world.

Serving businesses across South Africa, Kenya and worldwide.
