Brand identity

Algafusion

The architecture
of escape.

Luxury, local heritage and the natural world — brought together in an identity for a complete tourism experience.

Brand
Algafusion
Services
Logo design · Visual identity
Sector
Tourism & hospitality
Brand idea
The Architecture of Escape
01 / The idea

A complete escape.
A connected identity.

Algafusion’s brand rationale describes a 360-degree tourism experience that brings luxury, local heritage and the natural world together. Its promise to international travellers is a carefully crafted “rustic posh” adventure.

The Architecture of Escape gives that promise a clear expression. The identity connects the foundations of a campsite with the elevated experience of exclusive travel.

02 / The mark

From foundation
to apex.

The tiered A icon represents Algafusion’s vertically integrated model: from the physical foundations of its campsites to the apex of exclusive travel. The ascending form gives that idea a recognisable silhouette.

The presentation explores a horizontal wordmark and a compact stacked arrangement. Both pair the emblem with modern typography and the same tagline.

03 / Colour & pattern

Grounded in nature.
Lifted by orange.

The rationale pairs a Hermès-inspired orange with earthy greens, balancing the luxury positioning with a connection to outdoor landscapes. Cream and dark neutrals give the brighter colours room to stand out.

A repeating geometric pattern carries the palette beyond the logo. Its interlocking shapes bring detail to notebooks, stationery and small branded items.

04 / The identity in use

Small details.
A shared character.

The application board brings the identity into a coordinated stationery and digital concept: business cards, a letterhead, notebook, name tag, email signature, tags, stickers and a website preview.

Orange creates a focal point, while the pattern links the different formats. Generous neutral areas keep the logo and written information clear.

05 / The guest experience

From the first arrival
to the next adventure.

Four application concepts explore how the identity could carry through a guest’s stay: a lodge entrance sign, a welcome kit, staff uniforms and a safari touring vehicle.

Stone, cotton and timber keep the setting grounded. Orange and green give the emblem a clear presence, while the geometric pattern connects packaging, fabric and vehicle details.

06 / The world of the brand

An invitation
to step away.

The landscape compositions place the identity alongside a mountain lodge and an open-air campfire. Warm light and spacious imagery give The Architecture of Escape an atmospheric setting.

Together, the mark, palette and pattern create a consistent visual language across the presentation — from a small tag to a full landscape composition.

FAQ

For tourism & hospitality brands

Can Santi Universe design a brand for my lodge, camp or tourism business?

Yes. We design complete identities for tourism and hospitality brands — logo, colour palette, pattern and brand guidelines — built around the experience you offer. For Algafusion, the idea “The Architecture of Escape” shaped everything from the tiered A emblem to the geometric pattern.

Can you show how my brand will look on signage, uniforms and vehicles before we produce anything?

Yes. We create concept mockups of your identity on entrance signs, welcome kits, staff uniforms and vehicles, so you can see the whole guest experience before committing to production.

Do you build websites for lodges and travel businesses too?

Yes. We design and build fast, conversion-focused websites, and can add booking functionality with custom WordPress development. We also offer managed hosting and ongoing maintenance.
