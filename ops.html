How we run every project

For everyone who works on a client project. Watch this first. Everything in it is written out below: how we talk to clients, and each of the nine stages, with what to do in ERPNext.

How we talk

These are promises to the client, and the system is built to keep them. Anyone who writes to a client on behalf of Santi Universe writes this way.

  • A person answers, within one working day

    Every email and every request on the project page is read by a person. Working days are Monday to Friday, not South African public holidays.

    SayThanks, got it. I'll come back to you by tomorrow afternoon with a proper answer.

  • The project page is the record

    Dates, files, the brief, requests and money all live on the client's project page. Email is for conversation, and it is the only channel: no WhatsApp for client work. If something is agreed on a call, it goes onto the page the same day.

    SayI've added that to your project page, so it's there when you need it.

  • Mornings are for work, calls are Tuesday and Thursday afternoons

    Calls are 45 minutes at 13:00 or 15:00, booked at least a day ahead. At most two a day. Clients book them themselves from their project page.

    SayBook any Tuesday or Thursday slot on your project page and it's confirmed.

  • Name the number before the work

    New work is R850 an hour, or R500 an hour on a retainer. The client hears the price before any work or charge starts. Nobody finds out at invoice time.

    SayThat's new work: about 3 hours, so R2,550. Shall I add it to your invoice and schedule it?

  • Say who we are waiting on

    Every milestone is waiting on us, the client, or both. When it's us, we hold the dates. When it's the client, the dates move with them. Say it early and plainly, never as a complaint.

    SayWe're ready for your brand files. The dates after this move with them, so the sooner, the sooner.

  • Plain words, specific answers

    No jargon, no "circle back". A date, not "soon". A price, not "it depends". If we don't know yet, say when we will.

    SayI don't know yet. I'll know after Thursday's review and tell you then.

  • One round of changes, gathered together

    At review the client sends one consolidated list, not a stream of messages. It keeps changes complete and dates honest.

    SayCollect everything in one list and send it when you're done. We'll work through it in one go.

  • Good news gets said too

    A finished milestone is marked done the day it's done. The client's page celebrates it, and a short note from us makes it land.

    SayThe design direction is signed off. That was the big one. On to the build.

The journey, stage by stage

Who acts: Client Us Both Automatic
  1. 01

    Enquiry

    ClientUs
    The client
    • Fills in the contact form on santi.co.za.
    • Gets an automatic note straight away: a person will reply within one working day.
    By itself
    • A Lead is created in ERPNext. A repeat enquiry from the same email adds nothing new.
    • The acknowledgement goes out once per address, and repeats nothing they typed.
    • You get a "New lead" email.
    In ERPNext
    • Reply personally. Ask what you need to quote, or offer a Tuesday or Thursday call.
    • When they are going ahead: convert the Lead to a Customer, with a Contact that has their email. That email is how they sign in and where invoices go.

    PromiseA personal reply within one working day.

  2. 02

    Quote

    UsAutomatic
    The client
    • Gets "Your quote from Santi Universe" with a link to their quote page.
    • Sees every line, the total and the 50% deposit. The link works for 30 days.
    By itself
    • Submitting the Quotation sends the email and creates the private pay link.
    In ERPNext
    • Create a Quotation for the Customer and submit it.
    • Use the right service items. They decide the client's board: a website item gets the website milestones, a brand item gets the brand milestones.
    • Scope changed? Amend the Quotation. The client gets a fresh link.

    PromiseThe quote says exactly what they are buying, and nothing is added later without asking.

  3. 03

    Deposit

    ClientAutomatic
    The client
    • Pays the 50% deposit through Payfast from the quote page.
    • Lands on the Payment received page, then gets a receipt email with a Start your project button.
    By itself
    • The payment is checked with Payfast before anything is written.
    • ERPNext gets the Sales Order, the payment, the Project with its milestones, a deposit invoice marked Paid and a project invoice for the rest.
    • Telegram: PAID, then INVOICES OPENED.
    In ERPNext
    • Nothing, unless Telegram says something failed. Then follow the alert. The client has paid, so that comes first that day.

    PromiseThe moment they pay, they have a real project and a paid invoice.

  4. 04

    Onboarding

    ClientUs
    The client
    • Signs in to their project page with an email link. The link lasts 15 minutes, and they stay signed in for 30 days.
    • Watches the welcome video, fills in the 7-question brief, sends files (up to 8 MB each) and books the kickoff call.
    • If they give a deadline, the page tells them straight away how it compares with our plan.
    By itself
    • The brief is saved on the Project and "Brief received" is marked done.
    • The plan counts working days only, three of our tasks a day, two on call days.
    • A deadline tighter than the plan is offered a 20% rush fee. Ten or more spare working days earns 10% off.
    In ERPNext
    • Read the brief the day it arrives.
    • If a rush fee or discount applies and the client agrees, add it as a line on the project invoice.
    • Chase gently if nothing arrives within three working days.

    PromiseNothing is designed against a guess. The brief comes first.

  5. 05

    Kickoff call

    Both
    The client
    • Picks a Tuesday or Thursday slot and gets a confirmation email with what they wrote.
    • On the call we agree scope, priorities and dates.
    By itself
    • The call is added to ERPNext's calendar.
    In ERPNext
    • Mark "Kickoff booked" done after the call.
    • Put anything agreed on the Project the same day: changed dates, extra lines on the project invoice, notes.

    PromiseWhatever we agree on the call is written down where they can see it.

  6. 06

    The work

    UsClient
    The client
    • Follows along on the project page: one tab per milestone, plus Timeline, Board and Billing.
    • Can move the date of a milestone that is waiting on them; everything after it moves too.
    • Can ask for something new. The page shows the rate before they send.
    By itself
    • A request lands as a Task marked Pending Review. It is not on the plan until you accept it.
    • Anything added to the project invoice shows on their Billing tab the same day.
    In ERPNext
    • Mark each milestone Completed the day it's done. Their page celebrates it.
    • Answer every request within one working day. Already agreed: set it Open. New work: tell them the price, and once they say yes, add the line to the project invoice and set it Open. Not now: Cancelled, with a note why.

    PromiseThey always know what we're on, what's waiting on them, and what it costs.

  7. 07

    Review

    Client
    The client
    • Goes through the working site or the identity and sends one consolidated list of changes.
    By itself
    • The dates after Review wait for their list, and the Timeline shows it.
    In ERPNext
    • Confirm the list arrived and when the changes will be done.
    • Anything in the list that is new work follows the stage 6 rule: price first.

    PromiseOne complete round of changes, done properly.

  8. 08

    Launch and handover

    BothAutomatic
    The client
    • Gets the files, the access and a walkthrough.
    • Gets the final invoice by email: the whole job, the deposit already taken off, the balance due in 7 days, the banking details in the email.
    By itself
    • Setting the Project to Completed locks the project invoice, dates it today and emails it.
    • Telegram: INVOICED.
    In ERPNext
    • Before you mark it Completed, check the project invoice has every agreed extra on it.
    • Then set the Project status to Completed. Do it once, on the day of handover.

    PromiseThe invoice matches what was agreed, line for line.

  9. 09

    Paid and closed

    ClientUs
    The client
    • Pays by EFT with the invoice number as the reference.
    • Sees "Paid in full" on their Billing tab.
    By itself
    • Billing updates as soon as the payment is recorded against the invoice.
    In ERPNext
    • When the money arrives, record a Payment Entry against the project invoice.
    • Send a short thank-you. Ask for a review or a referral while it's fresh.

    PromiseA clean finish: paid, thanked, and welcome back any time.
