Brand identity concept

Slice Republic

A slice of character.
A touch of royalty.

A pizzeria identity exploration, from a crowned slice to the details of a takeaway experience.

In collaboration with

Scope
Logo · Colour · Typography · Brand applications
Sector
Pizzeria
Stage
Identity concept · Second-draft exploration
01 / The idea

Handcrafted flavour.
A distinctive mark.

The Slice Republic identity pairs an expressive pizza-slice emblem with a crown. The supplied guide connects the flowing forms to handcrafted pizza and the crown to a premium, welcoming experience.

Navy, gold and warm cream carry that idea across the wordmark, supporting marks and proposed brand applications.

02 / The exploration

Two directions.
One personality.

The second-draft presentation explores two wordmark treatments, labelled “Curves” and “Sharp Sharp”. Both retain the crowned slice, with differences in the letterforms giving each direction its own character.

Presented here as design development; the supplied documents do not establish which direction received final approval.

03 / The system

Deep blues.
Warm accents.

The guide specifies deep navy and royal blue alongside gold and cream. Light and dark logo treatments keep the identity recognisable across different backgrounds.

Arpona and Zeitung Pro are the typefaces named in the supplied identity guide.

  • Deep navy#153067
  • Royal blue#003B8B
  • Gold#B49255
  • Cream#E5CCAA
04 / In context

From the counter
to the takeaway.

The supplied mockups explore how the identity could appear in a pizzeria interior and on pizza boxes, drinks, bags and apparel. Repeated colours and the crowned-slice mark connect the different touchpoints.

05 / Further applications

More ways
to make an impression.

Four concept scenes extend the identity into pizza packaging, storefront signage, staff apparel and delivery. Navy, cream and gold connect each application to the crowned-slice mark.
