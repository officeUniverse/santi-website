How we run every project

For everyone who works on a client project. Watch this first. The same nine stages are written out below, for when you're in ERPNext.

How we talk

  • A person replies within one working day
  • The project page is the record
  • Calls on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons
  • The price comes before the work
  • Always say who we're waiting on

What we do in ERPNext, stage by stage

  1. 01

    The enquiry

    • Reply personally within one working day
    • Convert the Lead to a Customer
    • Put their email on the Contact
  2. 02

    The quote

    • Create a Quotation for the Customer
    • Pick the right service items
    • Submit it
    • Scope changed? Amend it
  3. 03

    The deposit

    • Nothing, unless an alert says a step failed
    • If one did, fix that first
  4. 04

    Onboarding

    • Read the brief the day it lands
    • Rush fee or discount agreed? Add it to the project invoice
    • Nothing in three working days? Chase gently
  5. 05

    The kickoff call

    • Mark Kickoff booked as Completed
    • Put what you agreed on the project, the same day
  6. 06

    The work

    • Mark each milestone Completed the day it's done
    • Answer every request within a day
    • New work: price, their yes, the invoice line, then Open
  7. 07

    Review

    • Confirm the list arrived, and when it'll be done
    • New work in the list? Price it first
  8. 08

    Handover

    • Check every agreed extra is on the project invoice
    • Set the Project status to Completed
    • Once, on handover day
  9. 09

    Paid and closed

    • Record a Payment Entry against the project invoice
    • Say thank you
    • Ask for a review or a referral
