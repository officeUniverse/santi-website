VISUAL IDENTITY & BRAND GUIDELINES

01 / CONTEXT

One group.
A connected identity.

Isiphethu operates across construction, engineering and water services. Its identity needs to remain recognisable across corporate communications and practical, everyday applications.

The brand guide brings the core elements together: logo construction, clear space, colour, typography and usage rules. The aim is a consistent presentation wherever the brand appears.

Explore Isiphethu ↗

02 / THE SYSTEM

Precision in
every detail.

A flowing blue symbol sits beside a distinctive italic wordmark. The guide defines how that relationship is protected through consistent proportions, clear space and minimum sizes.

Navy provides depth, mid-blue carries the primary colour, and pale blue creates contrast. Ubuntu and Montserrat are named as supporting typefaces in the guide.

03 / CORPORATE COMMUNICATION

Clear. Considered.
Recognisably Isiphethu.

The guide sets out a direct, clear voice, supported by values including teamwork, integrity, knowledge, reliability and innovation.

The stationery and company-profile concepts explore that identity in print, giving the logo room to stand out against clean surfaces and restrained blue details.

04 / BEYOND THE OFFICE

An identity
that travels.

A vehicle, workwear and a ceramic mug extend the visual system into everyday contexts. Each application keeps the logo legible and the palette consistent.

The six application images on this page are AI-generated concept mockups. They illustrate potential uses of the identity, rather than documenting a completed rollout.

05 / A BRAND FAMILY

Related services.
A shared foundation.

The guide also documents logo reconstruction studies for Engineering and Water Service. These explore how service-specific symbols can sit alongside the Isiphethu name.

They are presented here as development work from the guide, with the master identity providing the common foundation.
